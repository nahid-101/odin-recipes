Scrambled Eggs

Description

Scrambled eggs are one of the easiest and most satisfying dishes to make. Soft, fluffy, and buttery, they're perfect for breakfast, brunch, or even a quick snack. With just a few ingredients and a few minutes, you'll have a comforting meal on your plate.

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon butter (or 1 tablespoon oil)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a small bowl.
  2. Whisk them with a fork until the yolks and whites are fully combined.
  3. Place a nonstick pan on medium heat and melt the butter.
  4. Pour in the eggs and let them cook undisturbed for a few seconds.
  5. Gently stir with a spatula, pushing the eggs from the edges toward the center.
  6. Continue until the eggs form soft, fluffy curds.
  7. Remove from the heat while still slightly creamy (they'll finish cooking off the heat).
  8. Season with salt and black pepper, then serve immediately.
