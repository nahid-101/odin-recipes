Spaghetti

Description

A classic Italian pasta dish with rich tomato sauce and herbs.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil

Steps

  1. Boil spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. Heat tomato sauce with olive oil and seasonings.
  3. Mix spaghetti with sauce and garnish with basil.
