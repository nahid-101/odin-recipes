Fresh Salad

Description

A crisp, healthy salad with fresh vegetables and a light vinaigrette.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups lettuce
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1/2 cup olives
  • 2 tbsp olive oil and vinegar dressing

Steps

  1. Wash and chop all vegetables.
  2. Mix vegetables in a bowl.
  3. Drizzle with dressing and toss gently.
